1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This policy governs all outbound debt collection activity conducted by VOIZ agents on behalf of Predixion AI's lending partners. It applies to all loan products including personal loans, two-wheeler loans, and BNPL (Buy Now Pay Later) instruments. All agents — human and AI — must comply without exception.

2. DPD BUCKET DEFINITIONS AND TREATMENT RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

DPD (Days Past Due) buckets define the delinquency stage of an account and dictate the appropriate treatment strategy.

Bucket 0 (0 DPD — Current):
Account is current. No collections activity permitted. Agent may only call for payment reminders within 3 days of due date.

Bucket 1 (1–30 DPD — Early Delinquency):
Soft collections only. Agent must use empathetic tone. Maximum 2 calls per day. No field visits permitted. Offer EMI restructuring if borrower expresses hardship. Escalation to supervisor required if borrower is unresponsive after 5 attempts.

Bucket 2 (31–60 DPD — Mid Delinquency):
Standard collections. Maximum 3 calls per day. Field visit permitted after 10 unsuccessful call attempts. Legal notice may be issued after 45 DPD with supervisor approval. Settlement discount of up to 10% may be offered without supervisor approval.

Bucket 3 (61–90 DPD — Late Delinquency):
Intensive collections. Maximum 4 calls per day. Settlement discount of up to 20% permitted with supervisor approval. Legal action review mandatory at 90 DPD. Account flagged for NPA provisioning.

Bucket 4 (91+ DPD — NPA / Severely Delinquent):
Account classified as Non-Performing Asset. Legal action may proceed. Settlement negotiation governed by NPA Settlement Policy v1.3. AI agents must not engage NPA accounts without supervisor routing.

3. CALLING HOURS AND FREQUENCY RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Permitted calling hours: 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM local borrower time, Monday through Saturday.
Sunday calls: Prohibited unless borrower initiates contact.
Public holidays: Calls permitted only for Bucket 3 and Bucket 4 accounts.
Do Not Call (DNC) registry: Must be checked before every call. DNC accounts must not be dialled under any circumstances.
Consecutive day limit: No borrower may be called on more than 6 consecutive days without a 1-day gap.

4. SETTLEMENT GUIDELINES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Settlement offers must be based on the borrower's outstanding principal, accrued interest, and applicable penalty charges. The following discount bands are authorised:

- Bucket 1 accounts: No settlement discount permitted.
- Bucket 2 accounts: Up to 10% waiver on penalty charges only. Principal and interest not waivable.
- Bucket 3 accounts: Up to 20% waiver on penalty charges; up to 5% waiver on accrued interest. Supervisor approval required.
- Bucket 4 / NPA accounts: Settlement terms governed by NPA Settlement Policy v1.3. Case-by-case approval from Credit Committee required.

All settlement offers must be confirmed in writing within 24 hours via SMS and email to the registered contact details. Verbal settlements are not binding.

5. HARDSHIP POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A borrower claiming financial hardship must be transferred to the Hardship Support Team immediately. Hardship indicators include: job loss, medical emergency, death in immediate family, and natural disaster impact. The Hardship Support Team may approve:
- Payment pause of up to 90 days
- EMI reduction of up to 50% for up to 6 months
- Waiver of penalty charges for the hardship period

Hardship applications must be submitted within 30 days of the hardship event. Supporting documentation required within 15 days of application.

6. PROHIBITED CONDUCT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The following are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate escalation and audit:
- Threatening language, abusive or demeaning tone
- Misrepresenting the agent's identity or the consequences of non-payment
- Contacting third parties (employer, family, neighbours) without borrower consent
- Calling outside permitted hours
- Offering settlement terms beyond authorised discount bands without approval
- Collecting payment without issuing a receipt reference number
